Frequently asked questions

How do I know whether my documents are complete?
The assessment goes through every obligation: in place, missing or in progress, with the evidence expected and the action to take. The agent matches your documents against each requirement, and the compliance expert focuses on what is still missing.
How does it differ from CE marking?
The assessment covers all 9 obligation areas. CE marking is a separate service, from €3,800 per product.
Is laboratory testing included?
The assessment lists the tests to run. Testing is ordered separately from a laboratory.
Can Cleo complete what is missing?
Yes: every missing item points to the service that addresses it, from technical documentation to EU authorised representation.
How much does the assessment cost?
From €399 per product. Turnaround is stated before you order.
