Customs Classification API

A product description becomes explained candidate customs codes, so you can pre-classify your catalogue and isolate the cases to validate.

GET /v2/customs/lookup

GET /v2/customs/lookup?description=cotton+t-shirt&system=cn8&top=5
Host: api.legaldata.cleolabs.co
Authorization: Bearer $CLEO_API_KEY
You send
a product description, then country, language and nomenclature
Cleo returns
candidates with code, nomenclature, chapter, confidence level, rationale and an ambiguity flag
For
e-commerce, marketplaces, PIM, PLM, ERP, import-export teams and customs brokers
  • Nomenclatures served: HS6, EU CN8, Kazakhstan TN VED
  • Covered countries confirmed before the contract
  • Candidates to validate, never a binding ruling

Frequently asked questions

Which API should I choose?
Legal Data API if your software searches, reads or cites legal documents. Customs Classification API if your software needs to suggest a customs code from a product description.
Are the customs codes final?
They are explained candidates, with their confidence level and an ambiguity flag. Your team or your customs broker validates the code before the declaration.
Where do the Legal Data API documents come from?
Every document states its source and the URL of the original portal. The sources included in your access are named in your contract. Explore coverage.
How much does access cost?
A free Sandbox to test, Starter at €100 per month, Pro at €349 per month, Enterprise on quote. See pricing.
Can I use Legal Data in an AI assistant?
Yes, with the MCP server, using the same key as the API.
