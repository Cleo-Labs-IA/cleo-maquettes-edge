01 · Customs Classification API · In production

A product title is not a customs declaration. Turn it into a classification dossier.

Send the facts you have. Cleo asks for the ones that decide the code, cites the notes, and keeps the dossier your broker signs.

You send
a product, the facts you know, the country and the date
Cleo returns
the missing questions, or candidates down to the national line with cited evidence, and a JSON or PDF dossier
For
ERP and TMS vendors, e-commerce, PIM, 3PL, customs brokers
  • 112 countries at the national line out of 249, 26 nomenclatures
  • Batches of 2,000 items, webhooks, CSV write-back and an open-source Shopify connector
  • Candidates to validate, never a binding ruling

Frequently asked questions

Which API should I choose?
Legal Data API if your software searches, reads or cites legal documents. Customs Classification API if your software needs to suggest a customs code from a product description.
Are the customs codes final?
They are explained candidates, with their confidence level and an ambiguity flag. Your team or your customs broker validates the code before the declaration.
What can I test in the sandbox?
Every endpoint, within 200 units. Once you sign up, your dashboard lists each endpoint and what it costs. Get my sandbox key.
Where do the Legal Data API documents come from?
Every document states its source and the URL of the original portal. The sources included in your access are named in your contract. Explore coverage.
How much does access cost?
A free Sandbox with 200 units, Starter at €100 per month, Pro at €349 per month, Enterprise on quote. See pricing.
Can I use Legal Data in an AI assistant?
Yes, with the MCP server, using the same key as the API.
