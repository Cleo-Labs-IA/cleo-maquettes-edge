Our story

  • Anaëlle GuezCEO and co-founder
  • Naomie HaliouaCDO and co-founder
  • Alexandre BlochCTO

Cleo was born from a simple idea: put a lawyer and an engineer on the same problem. Anaëlle Guez, a graduate of the Sorbonne and Panthéon-Assas, and Naomie Halioua, who holds an MSc&T from École polytechnique, founded Cleo in 2023.

We watched every function of the company become automated, one after the other. Product compliance did not. It was still done by hand, handed question by question to outside advisers, market by market. The rules are many, they change, they overlap and they differ from one market to the next: knowing concretely what to do on a product was becoming almost impossible.

We first tried a general-purpose AI, built to answer everyone. It answered everyone a little, and no one well. So we reversed the method: real cases in real sectors, textiles first, then cosmetics and transport. It is slower than claiming to cover everything, and it is the only way to be right.

Today, Cleo encodes what matters most and tracks everything around it: the sources, the authorities, the draft texts and the consultations, verified by our team. Decathlon is one of our customers.

Mission

Make every rule accessible in seconds, and turn it into action on the product.

Our vision: a safer world, where everyone can access the rules written to protect people.

Our values

  • The source before the summary

    Every answer carries the text it rests on. It can be checked in seconds and defended in front of anyone.

  • Only what can be verified

    We refuse answers that sound right but cannot be verified. In this line of work, almost right is wrong.

  • The coverage we have built

    We only announce what we have built. Every market and every sector we open is built with the people who work in it.

  • For those who answer for the product

    Regulatory, quality, compliance, CSR and e-commerce teams. Not all of them lawyers, all of them responsible for what is placed on the market.

Our commitments

What we hold to for every file

  • 01

    Exhaustive

    Every rule that applies to your products is established, market by market, regulatory family by regulatory family.

  • 02

    Everything is traceable

  • 03

    Cleo tracks the sources continuously and flags every week what is about to change, before it enters into application.
