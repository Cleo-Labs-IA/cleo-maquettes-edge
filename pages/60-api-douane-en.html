How it works

  1. 1. Send the product

    One item or a batch of 2,000, by REST, TypeScript SDK or MCP.

  2. 2. Get the code, or the questions it still needs

    The level each country serves, and questions instead of guesses, with sources.

  3. 3. Approve, then write back

    Approved codes write back, and any code can be re-checked on a date.

Built to integrate

  • Batches report an error per line

What Cleo delivers

  • The coverage level of each answer: the national tariff line or 6 digits, stated in the response.
  • The tariff data version the answer read, with the date it takes effect.
  • A request id on every response and every error, to quote to support.
  • Idempotency keys: the same key and body within 24 hours replays the answer at no extra cost.

What stays in your hands

  • Your users’ customs broker or importer of record files the declaration. Cleo prepares the data.
  • An official customs ruling stays with the customs authority. Cleo delivers documented suggestions.
  • 112 of 249 territories answer at the national tariff line and 137 at 6 digits, and every answer states its level. In the US, the digits past 6 for an entry are set with a licensed customs broker. See the list
  • Every cost says what it includes. A duty Cleo could not price is named and the cost is marked partial, never guessed.

Questions

Is it predictable to integrate?
Yes. The guarantees listed under “How it works” are detailed in the documentation, with the webhook signature check.
Which countries?
112 of 249 territories answer at the national tariff line, the rest at 6 digits. Each answer says which level it used.
Is the code final?
No. It is a candidate with its sources, for your review. The importer, or the broker acting for them, decides what is declared.
What does a call cost?
One classification costs 1 unit, plus 1 per part of a kit. The Sandbox gives 200 units, no card.
