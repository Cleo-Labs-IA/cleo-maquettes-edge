I want to sell wireless earbuds in the US and in France. Which certifications are mandatory?

United States
  • FCC Part 15 (sub B) + Part 15.247 (Bluetooth)
  • FCC ID labelling required
  • Battery: UN38.3 + DOT
France (EU)
  • CE marking (RED 2014/53/EU)
  • RoHS 2011/65/EU + REACH SVHC
  • EU declaration of conformity

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Skills Library?
A free, open-source collection of 45 product compliance skills exposed through a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. Any compatible AI agent (Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT desktop or your own) can call them to answer regulatory questions on cosmetics, food, electronics, toys, textiles and supplements.
How do I install it?
One command: npx -y @cleo-labs/skills-mcp@latest. Add it to the config of your MCP client (Claude Code, Cursor, etc.) and the 45 skills are available right away.
Is it really free?
Yes. MIT licence. Use it in commercial products, reuse it, change it. We maintain it because we use it ourselves at Cleo.
What is the difference with Cleo?
The skills give the “what”: the knowledge of the regulations, frameworks and certifications. Cleo gives the “how”: your catalogue matched to the rules of 106 countries, tracked continuously, with audit evidence and team work.
Which MCP clients are supported?
Anything that speaks MCP over stdio. Tested with Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor and Continue. The server is a thin layer, so adapting it to a new client is mainly a matter of configuration.

MIT licence, no telemetry, no account.
