Frequently asked questions

What is Cleo Labs?
Cleo Labs automates product compliance for global brands. For each product and each market, Cleo tells you what applies, what is still missing and what to do, and a compliance expert reviews the file before handing it over. The company, Cleo Corp SAS, is based in Neuilly-sur-Seine, France.
Which regulations apply to my product?
It depends on the product and the markets where you sell it. A toy sold in the EU, for example, falls under Directive 2009/48/EC on the safety of toys and its EN 71 test standards. Describe your product: Cleo lists the legislation that applies, market by market, and cites each official text.
Which products and countries do you cover?
Consumer products, from toys to automotive, across 106 countries, inside and outside the EU. Cleo's base counts 25,000 regulations mapped, 19,000 regulatory authorities tracked and 3,700 official sources.
How is Cleo different from a regulatory watch service?
A watch service sends you texts. Cleo starts from your product: the rules that apply, the tests, the information to display and the supplier documents, brought together in one file that a compliance expert reviews.
How much does a service cost?
A price stated before you order, never by the hour: €399 per product for the assessment, from €1,200 per product for labelling, €3,800 per product for CE marking (testing excluded), €1,500 per year per category for the authorised representative.
Who signs the declaration of conformity?
The manufacturer, or its authorised representative. Cleo prepares the technical documentation and the draft EU declaration of conformity; a compliance expert reviews and validates it, and your organisation decides.
What is MARIA?
MARIA (Multi-Agent Regulatory Intelligence Architecture) is Cleo's multi-agent architecture. It is described in a research paper published in February 2026: 93 to 97% precision on binding texts, evaluated on 7,676 regulations.
Who are Cleo Labs' investors?
Cleo raised €1.5M. The round is led by Larry Berger, founder of Amplify, with participation from Kima Ventures and La Financière Saint-James.
Where is our data hosted?
With Scaleway, in the Paris region, in the European Union. Your data never trains a model.
