The four quadrants

The RegTech landscape can be mapped on two axes: reactive vs. proactive, and generic vs. company-specific. Traditional GRC platforms (OneTrust, Diligent) sit in the reactive-generic quadrant. Change management tools are reactive but more specific. Legal AI is emerging but still generic. The empty quadrant, proactive and company-specific, is where AI-first regulatory intelligence platforms like Cleo operate.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the RegTech market in 2026?
The global RegTech market is valued at approximately $42 billion in 2026, growing at a 15% CAGR. The regulatory intelligence and compliance automation segment represents roughly $12 billion. Growth is driven by regulatory complexity doubling every 5 years, AI Act and DORA enforcement, and enterprises shifting from manual compliance to AI-powered automation.
