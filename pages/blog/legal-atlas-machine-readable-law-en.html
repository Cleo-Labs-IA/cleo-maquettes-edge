The law is published. It is not accessible.

Every country publishes its legislation, but no two publish it the same way. A regulation might live as a scanned PDF on a ministry portal, a paginated HTML page on an official journal, or a database with no export. Citations differ, languages differ, update cadences differ. For a human researcher this is friction. For a software system (a legal-tech product, a compliance engine, an AI agent) it is a wall.

The result: the same legal text gets re-collected, re-parsed and re-verified by every team that needs it, over and over. Legal Atlas does that work once, at source, and keeps it fresh.

Frequently asked questions

What is Legal Atlas?
Legal Atlas is a machine-readable legal database by Cleo Labs. It aggregates and indexes legislation, case law and doctrine from authenticated official government sources, and exposes them through a single REST API. It currently tracks 1,494 official sources across 177 jurisdictions.
Who is it for?
Legal-tech SaaS companies, law firms running research at scale, and teams building AI agents that need to reason over real, current law. There is an interactive web playground to explore the data before integrating the API.
How can I access it?
The interactive playground is open at legaldata-public.cleolabs.co with no signup required. Programmatic access comes in two plans: Pro (120 requests/minute) and Enterprise (600 requests/minute with an SLA and dedicated support).
