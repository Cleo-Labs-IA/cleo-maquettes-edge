The old model is broken

For decades, compliance monitoring meant subscribing to regulatory newsletters, setting keyword alerts on official gazettes, and assigning analysts to read, interpret, and flag relevant changes. This approach has fatal flaws: volume overwhelms analysts (3,700+ regulatory sources publish daily), context is lost (keyword matches don't understand relevance), and response time is too slow (days or weeks between publication and action).

Contextual intelligence: the paradigm shift

AI-powered monitoring doesn't just detect that a regulatory change occurred. It understands what it means for you. When the EDPB publishes new guidance on cross-border transfers, a contextual AI system instantly determines: does this apply to our company? Which business units are affected? What obligations change? What is the deadline?

This is the difference between receiving 200 alerts a week (99% irrelevant) and receiving 5 actionable intelligence briefings with risk scores, affected teams, and recommended actions.
