Cleo Labs wins the Scaleway Startup Challenge and joins VivaTech 2026

Cleo Labs is the winner of the Scaleway Startup Challenge at VivaTech 2026 (“product compliance for global brands”): up to €250K in cloud credits and dedicated Scaleway support. Meet the team on the Scaleway booth, June 17–20, Hall 7.3 – Booth 3F10, Porte de Versailles, Paris.

What the win includes

As winner of the Scaleway Startup Challenge, Cleo Labs receives :

  • Up to €250K in cloud credits to scale its multi-agent regulatory intelligence platform.
  • Dedicated support from the Scaleway teams.
  • A place alongside Scaleway at VivaTech and at aiPULSE 2026, Scaleway’s flagship AI event.

Thanks to the jury members and to VivaTech for supporting this initiative : Jean-Baptiste Kempf, Jean-Baptiste Fourmont, Alexis Robert and Georges Abi-Aad.

Meet Cleo Labs at VivaTech

The Cleo Labs team will be on the Scaleway booth throughout the event, with a live walkthrough of the platform: from pre-launch regulatory mapping to continuous monitoring across markets.

Dates : June 17–20, 2026

Location : Hall 7.3: Booth 3F10, Porte de Versailles, Palais des expositions, Paris

Cleo Labs is powered by MARIA (Multi-Agent Regulatory Intelligence Architecture), a proprietary multi-agent AI pipeline that continuously monitors more than 25,000 regulatory authorities across 106 countries, with every analysis validated by legal experts through a human-in-the-loop approach.
