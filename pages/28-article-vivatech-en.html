Cleo wins the Scaleway Startup Challenge and joins VivaTech 2026

Winner in the « product compliance for global brands » category, with up to €250K in cloud credits and dedicated support from the Scaleway teams.

Naomie Halioua CDO and co-founder 15 June 2026 · 3 minute read

Cleo, which automates product compliance for international brands, has been named winner of the Scaleway Startup Challenge at VivaTech 2026. Among the competing startups, Cleo was selected in the « product compliance for global brands » category.

What the win includes

  • Up to €250K in cloud credits to scale Cleo's multi-agent architecture
  • Dedicated support from the Scaleway teams
  • A place alongside Scaleway at VivaTech and at aiPULSE 2026

Thanks to the jury members and to VivaTech for their support: Jean-Baptiste Kempf, Jean-Baptiste Fourmont, Alexis Robert and Georges Abi-Aad.

Meeting Cleo at VivaTech

The team will be on the Scaleway stand throughout the event, with a live demonstration of Cleo: from pre-launch regulatory mapping to continuous monitoring market by market.

  • Dates: 17 to 20 June 2026
  • Venue: Hall 7.3, booth 3F10, Porte de Versailles, Paris
