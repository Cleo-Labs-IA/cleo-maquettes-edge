Cleo raises €1.5M to automate product regulatory compliance at a global scale

Round led by Larry Berger, with participation from Kima Ventures, Financière Saint-James, and several prominent figures from the tech ecosystem.

Naomie Halioua CDO and co-founder 29 April 2026 · 4 minute read

A challenge intensifying with global regulatory complexity

International companies face a fragmented and constantly changing regulatory environment. Launching a product such as a connected bike helmet can involve more than a hundred different regulations, covering material standards, country-specific certifications, labelling requirements and customs constraints.

Automating product compliance at a global scale

Cleo runs on a multi-agent architecture that tracks 19,000 regulatory authorities across 106 countries. A compliance expert reviews and validates every analysis.

  • Pre-launch mapping producing the list of regulatory requirements per product and per market
  • Continuous monitoring, with each change tied to the products it touches

A first round to structure growth

The round is led by Larry Berger, with participation from Kima Ventures and La Financière Saint-James. Cleo also won The Pitch by Deel, qualifying the company for the international final in Dubai. The funds support European expansion and preparation for the US market.

Founders at the crossroads of tech and law

Founded by Naomie Halioua, who holds an MSc&T from École polytechnique, and Anaëlle Guez, a graduate of the Sorbonne and Panthéon-Assas.
