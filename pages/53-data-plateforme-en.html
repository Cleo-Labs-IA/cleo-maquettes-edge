Frequently asked questions

How reliable is the data?
Cleo's architecture is described in a research paper published in February 2026: 93 to 97% precision on binding texts, evaluated on 7,676 regulations.
Do I need to check the platform every day?
No. Cleo follows the sources continuously and flags what will change before it takes effect.
