In short

An authorised representative is a person established in the Union whom a manufacturer appoints by written mandate to act on its behalf. Since 13 December 2024, every product covered by Regulation (EU) 2023/988 has an operator established in the Union that answers for it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an EU authorised representative?
You need an operator established in the Union: the importer, the authorised representative or, failing both, the fulfilment service provider (Regulation (EU) 2023/988, Article 16).
Authorised representative or responsible person: what is the difference?
The responsible person is the operator established in the Union that answers for the product. The authorised representative is one of the persons who can hold that role.
What stays with the manufacturer?
The design of the product and the technical documentation (Decision No 768/2008/EC, Annex I, Article R3).
How long are the documents kept?
10 years after placing on the market for radio equipment (Directive 2014/53/EU, Article 11). The period depends on the text.
Who signs the declaration?
The manufacturer, or its authorised representative. The Cleo expert reviews and validates the declaration.
Is the representative liable for a defective product?
Yes for products placed on the market after 9 December 2026, when the manufacturer is established outside the Union (Directive (EU) 2024/2853, Article 8).
How much does the authorised representative cost?
€1,500 per year per product category, answers to authorities included. If your products fall into several categories, the price follows their number and the total is stated before you order.
