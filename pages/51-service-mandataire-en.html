In short

An authorised representative is a person established in the Union whom a manufacturer appoints by written mandate to act on its behalf for specified tasks. Since 13 December 2024, a product covered by Regulation (EU) 2023/988 is placed on the market with an economic operator established in the Union that is responsible for it. For a manufacturer established outside the Union, that role goes to the importer, the authorised representative or, failing both, the fulfilment service provider. This operator keeps the declaration of conformity available to authorities, makes sure the technical documentation can be provided on request, answers reasoned requests, and its contact details appear on the product, its packaging, the parcel or an accompanying document.

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need an EU authorised representative?
You need an operator established in the Union. For a manufacturer established outside the Union, that role goes to the importer, the authorised representative or, failing both, the fulfilment service provider (Regulation (EU) 2023/988, Article 16, and Regulation (EU) 2019/1020, Article 4).
Authorised representative or responsible person: what's the difference?
Regulation (EU) 2023/988 speaks of the responsible person: the economic operator established in the Union that answers for the product. The authorised representative is one of the persons who can hold that role, alongside a manufacturer established in the Union, the importer and the fulfilment service provider.
What does the representative do, and what stays with the manufacturer?
The representative performs the tasks listed in the written mandate: documents available, answers to authorities, cooperation. The design of the product and the drawing up of the technical documentation stay with the manufacturer (Decision No 768/2008/EC, Annex I, Article R3).
How long are the documents kept?
For the period set by each text. For radio equipment, 10 years after it is placed on the market (Directive 2014/53/EU, Article 11).
Who signs the declaration?
The manufacturer, or its authorised representative. The Cleo expert reviews and validates the declaration, which is handed over to you ready to sign.
Is the representative liable for a defective product?
For products placed on the market after 9 December 2026, yes, when the manufacturer is established outside the Union: Directive (EU) 2024/2853, Article 8, makes the importer and the manufacturer's authorised representative liable, without prejudice to the manufacturer's own liability.
