    Hydraulic fluid power

    Rationale. Heading 84136031 covers hydraulic fluid power pumps, which directly matches this gear pump's primary function of providing hydraulic power transmission. The cast iron body and 250 bar rating are typical specifications for hydraulic pumps classified under this heading.
