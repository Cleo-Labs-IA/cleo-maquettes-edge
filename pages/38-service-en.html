Frequently asked questions

Who signs the declaration?
The manufacturer, or its authorised representative. The Cleo expert reviews and validates the updated declaration, then hands it over to you ready to sign.
Does my product need a declaration?
It depends on the legislation that applies to the product. The second step of the service identifies them, market by market.
