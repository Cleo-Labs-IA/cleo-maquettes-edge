About the role

At Cleo Labs, we help brands launch their products in new countries without being blocked by regulation, and stay compliant over time. Before a brand sells a product in a country, Cleo identifies the rules that apply: PFAS, REACH, Digital Product Passport, origin, traceability. Then we monitor continuously: as soon as a rule moves, we alert the team and flag what needs to change, a formula, a label, a certification. Major brands already trust us.

We are now looking for the person who will bridge law, customers and our technology.

Your mission, customer side

  • Support each customer: understand their needs, their products and their stakes, and help them get the most out of Cleo day to day.
  • Interpret their product ranges and the regulations that apply to them, in every country, and settle the tricky cases.
  • Be their trusted point of contact on regulatory matters, from onboarding to long-term follow-up.
  • Guarantee that what we deliver is right, from R&D to labelling to customs.

Your mission, product side

  • Select and consolidate the regulatory sources that feed our platform.
  • Verify and improve the quality of what our AI produces, so that every answer holds up legally.
  • Pass on your expertise so it becomes part of the product.

The profile

  • 5 to 10 years in product compliance or regulatory law, ideally in-house. Time spent in a luxury house, retail or consumer goods is a real asset, but we keep an open mind.
  • A strong command of product regulations (composition, labelling, safety, traceability, PFAS, REACH, Digital Product Passport...), in Europe and ideally beyond.
  • You like to structure, clarify and set clear rules, not only give case-by-case opinions.
  • Autonomous: you can take a fuzzy topic and move it forward on your own.
  • A genuine curiosity for artificial intelligence and what it can bring to the legal craft.
