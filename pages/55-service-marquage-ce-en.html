Frequently asked questions

How long does CE marking take?
It depends on the product and the tests required. Turnaround is stated before you order.
Does my product need CE marking?
Yes, if a directive or regulation that requires it applies to your product: for an electrical or electronic product, often electromagnetic compatibility, low voltage, radio equipment and RoHS. The product compliance assessment tells you for your product.
CE marking or CE certification?
The CE marking is affixed by the manufacturer or its authorised representative (Regulation (EC) No 765/2008, Article 30). For low-voltage electrical equipment, conformity is shown through internal production control, without a notified body (Directive 2014/35/EU, Annex III).
Is laboratory testing included in the €3,800?
Testing is billed separately by the laboratory, depending on your product. The €3,800 covers everything else: technical documentation, safety assessment, declaration, test coordination and platform access.
Who signs the EU declaration of conformity?
The manufacturer, or its authorised representative. The Cleo expert reviews and validates the declaration, ready to sign.
