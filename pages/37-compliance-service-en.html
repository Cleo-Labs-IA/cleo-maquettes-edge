Frequently asked questions

Who signs the declaration?

The manufacturer, or its authorised representative. For CE marking, Cleo prepares the technical documentation and the draft EU declaration of conformity, which you sign; the Cleo expert reviews and validates it.

Which documents do I need, are they complete, are they valid?

The product compliance assessment, from €399 per product: for each obligation, the evidence required, what is in place, what is still missing and what to do.

How much does a service cost?

A price stated before you order, never by the hour: from €399 per product for the assessment, from €1,200 per product for labelling, from €3,800 per product for CE marking (testing excluded) and from €1,500 per year per category for the authorised representative.

My service is not listed?

An expert looks at your catalogue with you.

Which products do you cover?

Consumer products, not just one family: from toys to automotive.

What is included?

Monitoring: Cleo flags every week the legislation that affects your products.

And for selling outside the EU?

Cleo tracks 90 jurisdictions, inside and outside the EU. For a new country, an expert looks at your catalogue with you.

What if my supplier does not give me the information?

For CE marking, Cleo requests and verifies the documents with your suppliers.

How do I check the agent's work?

Every answer cites the official text, and a compliance expert reviews and validates every deliverable before handover.

Where is our data stored?

Your data stays hosted in the European Union and never trains a model.

A new country is no longer a project. It is a decision.
