What Cleo delivers

  • The missing product facts, as questions you can forward to your supplier.
  • Your catalog classified for each destination, from a Shopify pull or a CSV, in batches of up to 2,000 items.
  • A review step: you approve each code before it is used.
  • Write-back to Shopify or your CSV of approved codes only, never over a different code already there.

What stays in your hands

  • Your customs broker or importer of record files the declaration. Cleo prepares the data.
  • An official customs ruling stays with the customs authority. Cleo delivers documented suggestions.
  • 112 of 249 territories answer at the national tariff line and 137 at 6 digits, and every answer states its level. In the US, the digits past 6 for an entry are set with a licensed customs broker. See the list
  • Every cost says what it includes. A duty Cleo could not price is named and the cost is marked partial, never guessed.

Questions

Will the duty at checkout be right?
The cost lists the parts it includes and names the parts it could not price. The carrier’s final bill and your checkout settings stay outside the API.
Does it change my Shopify products?
Only when you run the write-back, only with codes you approved, and never over a different code already there.
Is the code final?
No. It is a candidate with its sources, for your review. The importer, or the broker acting for them, decides what is declared. For a US entry, the full 10-digit line is set with your licensed customs broker.
What does a call cost?
One classification costs 1 unit, plus 1 per part of a kit. The Sandbox gives 200 units, no card.
