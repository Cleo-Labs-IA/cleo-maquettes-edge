A multi-agent pipeline approach to regulatory intelligence
Naomie Halioua, Alexandre Bloch, Anaelle Guez · Cleo Labs · February 2026

We formalise regulatory intelligence as a distinct computational problem and present a production system that, from a company domain, identifies the applicable regulations across 19 regions and 16 sectors, discovers developments in 8 languages, scores the results on a risk matrix, and generates impact assessments, orchestrating 30+ specialised LLM calls and one web search per jurisdiction and language.

Search infrastructure

A single pipeline run breaks down into one targeted search per jurisdiction and language.

Stotal ≤ (20 + ε) + (C × 4) + 5 · Nreg

where Nreg is the number of regulations passing the relevance filter, ε ∈ [0, 28] captures the agentic follow-up searches, and C the number of regulatory blocks retained from a set of 37 (19 geographic, 2 cross-cutting, 16 sectoral), with C ≤ 37.

Streaming architecture

Callback streaming removes the batch bottlenecks. Articles travel through the pipeline one by one as soon as they are ready.

Batch (traditional)
T = Σ max(tij)

Each stage waits for every article to have been processed before the next one starts.

Streaming (Cleo)
T ≈ max(Σ tij)

Each article moves through scoring, enrichment and assessment as soon as it is ready.
