Mission

Turn the rule into something a machine can act on.

Every other function of the company runs on structured data. Product compliance still runs on documents read by hand. We encode the obligation itself and keep the wording of the official source with it, so a team checks a product instead of rereading a text.

Vision

Compliance as an infrastructure layer.

Current, sourced, and callable from the systems you already run: the PLM, the product record, the supplier questionnaire. You open a market because the answer is there, not because someone found the time to look for it.
